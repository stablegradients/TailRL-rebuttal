MaxRL is TailRL’s special case for binary rewards (Sec. 3.2). MaxRL itself says it “does not extend directly to continuous or arbitrarily valued rewards” (its Sec. 8). TailRL and MaxRL share a likelihood-based view of reward optimization, but TailRL is not a straightforward extention to continuous rewards.
